# NEXUS
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript ES modules, Node.js 24+ standard-library local server and SQLite. No npm dependencies or external database service. Local private credentials/device token/signing identity are generated at first startup.

## Users and purpose
Township managers inspect/rank resource opportunities, test economics, approve coordinated work and review evidence. Facilities staff record work and imports. Residents contribute BioLoop material or confirm a shared trip; auditors inspect signed evidence. Sense → Optimise → Mobilise → Verify spans Water + BioLoop, Energy scheduling and RideShare. Seri Murni is a fictional site.

## Operating context
Sime Darby Property hackathon, PLANET focus. Organiser clarification allows public/research and clearly disclosed synthetic data; no proprietary datasets are provided. Broader Malaysian township applicability matters. User asks for a polished dynamic application, readable type, purposeful action motion and genuine public live inputs. English product copy is an agent implementation assumption.

## Capabilities and constraints
Four original Water views retain schematic map/pan/zoom/timeline, allocation/mulch/transport/cost comparison, resident collection/staff weighing, raw/ready inventory separation, interruption/cancellation and outcome accounting. Scenarios: public Live outlook plus seasonal, +30% dry-spell, dry reference and monsoon stress. Opportunity queue computes four constrained candidates; Water routes are exclusive alternatives. Evidence readiness is a completeness/source/freshness rules index, never statistical confidence.

Energy compares 24 hourly loads/PV, respects declared equipment windows/grid limits, freezes a human-approved schedule and reconciles labelled synthetic or supplied cycle data. No energy trading/rewiring/CREAM settlement. RideShare opens a bounded school/office or forecast-rain mission; distinct resident participants attest the same trip before once-only credits. One-pair pilot, no GPS/independent trip proof.

Guest demo persists separately in localStorage. Authenticated manager/staff/resident/auditor permissions are server-enforced with CSRF, optimistic revisions, transactions and once-only award keys. SQLite signed/hash-linked append-only history retains resets/cycles. CSV/device-token ingestion validates contracts and provenance. Supplied tank/quality/route references require manager review; imported results remain user-supplied comparisons, not causal savings.

## Evidence on hand
Actual Open-Meteo current-model weather/rain/ET₀/irradiance and MET district context; retained ERA5 July 2025 climate, published Air Selangor tariff and FAO guidance. Public forecasts update pending drafts, approval freezes inputs. No physical meter/provider, actual lab/permission records, measured site outcomes or interviews were supplied. Stocks, loads, routes, costs, effect sizes, processing assumptions and demo identities remain disclosed. Missing feeds stay missing; simulated batches never become live observations.

## Principles
Readable operational hierarchy and native controls. Purposeful immediate action feedback with keyboard/reduced-motion support. Hard constraints before scoring; negative economics remain visible. Never add unlike units or invent carbon/savings. Separate booked/checked-in from staff-weighed/awarded. A signed chain is tamper-evident under a pinned identity, not immutable against a host administrator with the key. Prototype runs loopback only; it is not production SaaS, real dispatch, payments or a regulated transport/water certification.

Map/action/wallet motion and interactive chart/form controls explain state. Desktop module links become a native selector on mobile; original four Water navigation destinations remain available.
