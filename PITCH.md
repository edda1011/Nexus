## Three-minute spoken script

**0:00–0:25 — Problem and purpose**

“Townships can have water, material and people available, yet still miss the next useful action because their work is disconnected. NEXUS connects resource signals to a feasible plan, a responsible operator and a checkable outcome. Our demonstration community is fictional; public weather is live model data, and every site assumption is labelled.”

**0:25–0:55 — Sense and optimise**

“Here, public rainfall and evapotranspiration update irrigation demand. A separate synthetic weekly fixture demonstrates a 25-cubic-metre spike. The opportunity queue checks physical limits before comparing impact, cost and evidence. We can inspect each source and timestamp. A dedicated transfer may cost more than the water benefit, so the interface shows that trade-off instead of hiding it.”

**0:55–1:35 — Mobilise**

“Using the repeatable dry reference, the manager reviews the calculation and approves. The approved inputs freeze. Work orders coordinate inspection, water transfer and existing processed mulch. A resident books a collection and checks in, but receives no credit yet. Only staff-confirmed weight awards credits once. New raw material replenishes a future cycle; it does not instantly become this intervention's mulch. In the authenticated workspace, manager, staff and resident actions have separate server permissions.”

**1:35–2:05 — Verify**

“After the work, we compare predicted and synthetic outcomes. This dry fixture reconciles 3.33 cubic metres of reduced demand and 36 of water substitution. That is a demonstration, not measured savings. The prototype also accepts labelled real meter exports and produces signed evidence for independent checking. Signatures protect traceability under the pinned identity; they do not make a physical claim true.”

**2:05–2:35 — Extend**

“Energy moves eligible loads toward a public irradiance forecast while preserving total energy and equipment limits. RideShare creates a bounded mission, then requires distinct driver and passenger attestations before demo rewards. We clearly separate these pilot mechanisms from electricity trading, GPS verification or a funded transport service.”

**2:35–3:00 — Value and next step**

“Our next step is one approved landscape pilot: measured stock, quality and route evidence, baseline and follow-up meters, full operating costs and an independent reviewer. Once that loop demonstrates repeatable value, the same contracts can support other Malaysian communities. We are seeking a pilot operator and feedback on integration and adoption.”

## Backup slides / likely questions

**Is this just a dashboard?** Show the before/after workflow: constraint → reviewed action → role-specific work → once-only incentive → outcome comparison. Charts are inputs to that mechanism.

**What is real today?** Public weather/irradiance and retained published references. Site stock, costs, effect sizes and demo outcomes are assumptions. CSV/provider ingestion is implemented; no physical device has been supplied or connected. No real customer interviews are claimed.

**How do you avoid double counting?** Shared/dedicated routes are the same Water intervention; reservations are within that plan. Demand reduction and substitution are separate. Raw versus ready material, planned versus delivered volume and credits versus physical outcomes remain separate. Server award keys prevent replay.

**Why no blockchain/LLM?** The proposal allows structured explanations. Deterministic constraints are inspectable and reproducible. Ed25519/hash-linked exports support tamper evidence; independent key pinning matters more than calling a local trail immutable. Neither technology proves field execution.

**What about financial value?** Show Shared/Dedicated and additional-cost sensitivity. Some current public forecasts produce negative net benefits. Pilot prices, installation costs, willingness to pay and annual savings need real validation; no invented ROI.

**What about scale and governance?** Refer to PILOT.md: owner/quality/consent, source contracts, managed identity, scoped devices, isolation, backups and independent checkpoints. Current deployment is local single-site.
