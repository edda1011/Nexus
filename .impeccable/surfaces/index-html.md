Scope: the code-led buildless v0.5 web prototype. Four Water + BioLoop hash views (overview, Action studio, Community and Impact ledger) are joined by Opportunity queue, Energy, RideShare and Connections & audit. Visitor mode is Operate. The desktop audience is a township manager comparing one Water + BioLoop intervention; Community demonstrates a resident wallet and staff hand-off.

The job is to inspect a resource connection, test allocation and cost assumptions, approve a feasible plan, and follow its operational/accounting trail. Overview presents balances above a schematic map and linked inspector. Action studio places live estimates beside controls and work orders. Community puts the wallet first on a phone. The ledger shows the same intervention with source classes and an evidence download.
